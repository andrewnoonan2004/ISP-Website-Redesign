Plan your application

Use these steps for MS or PhD study in Intelligent Systems. The official ISP pages below provide application guidance; the School of Computing and Information (SCI) admissions team can confirm details for your intended entry term.

  1. Choose your degree and review requirements. Compare the 30-credit MS and 72-credit PhD requirements → and browse the 2026–2027 courses and prerequisites explorer →.
  2. Review the required materials. Check the official ISP application requirements ↗ for documents, recommendations, and test policies that apply to you.
  3. Confirm deadlines, scholarships, and funding. Read the official application procedures ↗, explore ISP scholarships, fellowships, and funding →, and confirm dates and funding consideration for your degree and entry term with SCI admissions.
  4. Follow the application instructions. Open SCI admissions ↗ and choose “Apply to Graduate Programs” to reach the application portal.

Financial support for graduate applicants

Scholarships, fellowships, and financial aid

Gaining admission to the Intelligent Systems Program is a separate process from receiving financial aid, though once a candidate is admitted to ISP, the program makes every effort to identify a source of support (legacy priority deadline for financial aid consideration: January 15; TODO: confirm current entry-term priority deadline with sciadmit@pitt.edu).

  • PhD fellowships & assistantships: Incoming PhD applicants are automatically considered for SCI Predoctoral Fellowships (no separate application form required). PhD students are also supported through Graduate Research Assistantships (GSR) funded by ISP faculty PIs, Provost Predoctoral Fellowships (typically after Year 2), Summer GSA Fellowships, the Elizabeth U. Baranger Predoctoral Fellowship, the Provost’s Dissertation Completion Fellowship, and the DBMI Training Grant (for eligible MD holders).
  • MS scholarships & Pitt2Pitt: The MS in Intelligent Systems (MSISP) is a verified participating program in the Pitt2Pitt Scholarship for Pitt undergraduates and alumni (up to $7,500/year full-time or $3,750/year part-time). MS applicants can also review SCI Graduate Scholarships (Barbara E. King, Russell “Rusty” A. Howard II, and Jerry & Cathy Smith awards) and PittFund$Me. Per SCI guidance, program-funded assistantships and full fellowships for MS students are rare.
  • ISP Student Travel Grants ($750–$1,000/year): All enrolled MS and PhD students presenting an accepted paper or poster at an AI conference can apply for $750–$1,000 per academic year in travel support.
