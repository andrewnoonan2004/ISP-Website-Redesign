Admissions

Explore graduate study and connect with SCI admissions.

Plan your application

Use these steps for MS or PhD study in Intelligent Systems. The official ISP pages below provide application guidance; the School of Computing and Information (SCI) admissions team can confirm details for your intended entry term.

  1. Choose your degree. Compare the MS and PhD programs →.
  2. Review the required materials. Check the official ISP application requirements ↗ for documents, recommendations, and test policies that apply to you.
  3. Confirm deadlines and funding. Read the official application procedures ↗, then confirm dates and funding consideration for your degree and entry term with SCI admissions.
  4. Follow the application instructions. Open SCI admissions ↗ and choose “Apply to Graduate Programs” to reach the application portal.

Open official application instructions ↗

Ask about your application

For deadline, eligibility, document, or funding questions, email SCI admissions at sciadmit@pitt.edu. Include your intended degree (MS or PhD) and entry term so the team can give you relevant guidance.

Confirm before applying. This preview does not establish deadlines, funding eligibility, or admission requirements. Use official University guidance and ask SCI admissions about any conflicting or unclear information.
