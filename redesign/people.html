Hang Zhang is a PhD student of the Intelligent Systems Program at the University of Pittsburgh, advised by Dr. Yanshan Wang. His research focuses on AI Agent and AI in healthcare. His work explores how we can improve the accuracy, reliability, and practical applicability of Agentic AI systems in real-world medical and clinical settings.
