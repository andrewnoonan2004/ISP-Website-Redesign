ISP Faculty Members Ranked Among Top 2% of Scientists

Content from the supplied ISP website snapshot.

Back to news

Historical archive from the supplied ISP website snapshot. Dates and descriptions refer to the original publication; this is not a current announcement or live feed.

Several ISP faculty members were ranked among the top 2% of scientists in the world in the 2024 Stanford/Elsevier Top 2% Scientists List.

The list, created by Stanford University with data provided by Elsevier, shows the top 2% of scientists across a range of scientific fields. Updated annually, the list draws from a variety of citation metrics to determine inclusion.

ISP faculty included in the list:

  • Kevin Ashley (70,126)
  • Peter Brusilovsky (38,444)
  • Bruce Buchanan (95,585)
  • Jeffrey Cohn (21,627)
  • Gregory Cooper (49,931)
  • Milos Hauskrecht (149,221)
  • Michael Lewis (96,711)
  • Diane Litman (108,642)
  • Christian Schunn (49,774)

Please join us in congratulating these ISP faculty members on this achievement!
