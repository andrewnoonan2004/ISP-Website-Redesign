ISP’s  Computer Vision Research group works with automatic understanding of Image and Video

Researchers in this group focus on several different areas, including:

  • The research group works with various aspects of visual rhetorics in media, visual advertisements and visual annotations
  • The research groups also includes Affect Analysis Group, which primarily focuses on automatic analysis and synthesis of facial expression and prosody
  • The group also focuses on Computational Biomedical imaging analysis, Imaging-based clinical studies and Radiomics/Radiogenomics
