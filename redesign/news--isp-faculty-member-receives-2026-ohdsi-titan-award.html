ISP Faculty Member Receives 2026 OHDSI Titan Award

Archived ISP news and events.

Back to news

Historical archive. Dates and descriptions refer to the original publication, not upcoming events or current announcements.

Dr. Richard Boyce; an associate professor for the Department Biomedical Informatics with secondary appointments in the Clinical Translational Science Institute, the School of Pharmacy, and the Intelligent Systems Program; received the Titan Award for Open-Source Analytics at the 2026 Observational Health Data Science and Informatics (OHDSI) Global Symposium. 

Introduced at the 2018 Symposium, the Titan Awards recognize OHDSI collaborators and collaborating institutions for their contributions toward OHDSI's mission. The Open-Source Analytics Award recognizes extraordinary contributions by an individual in design, development, testing, and deployment of open-source software to enable observational analyses.

Please join us in congratulating Dr. Boyce on this achievement!
