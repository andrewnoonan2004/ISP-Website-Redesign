2026–2027 catalog updates & open verification items

This course explorer reconciles the legacy ISP website and 2026 MS/PhD program guides against the official 2026–2027 University of Pittsburgh Graduate Catalog (catoid=242):

1. MS General Track Core update (Human Language Technologies): In the 2026–2027 MS catalog (catoid=242&poid=80371), Human Language Technologies replaces the older Introductory Natural Language Processing listing in the MS General Track Core “choose two” options, while Introductory Natural Language Processing remains an Approved Advanced Course for both MS and PhD students.

2. Theory — Statistics update (Applied Regression Analysis): In the 2026–2027 MS and PhD catalogs (catoid=242), Applied Regression Analysis in Biostatistics replaces the older Introductory Statistical Methods 2 listing in the Theory — Statistics “choose one” requirement group.

3. Six new 2026–2027 Approved Advanced Courses: The 2026–2027 catalog adds graduate electives in Introduction to Deep Learning, Network Science and Analysis, FATE: Fairness, Accountability, Transparency, and Ethics in AI, Applied Legal Data Analytics and AI, Principles of Data Mining, and (for PhD students) Advanced Topics in Data Mining: Social and Heterogeneous Graph Mining.

Permission numbers, outside-SCI contacts & cross-registration

Some curriculum courses are administered by other SCI departments or other schools at Pitt and carry degree-plan restrictions (such as Computer Science, Computer Engineering, or Biomedical Informatics). When PeopleSoft shows a plan restriction or department consent requirement, follow these steps:

1. Request written instructor approval first: Email the course instructor to request enrollment permission, and include your 7-digit PeopleSoft ID and the 5-digit class number from PeopleSoft.

2. SCI courses (including Full-Time Dissertation Study and Summer Milestone Study): Submit your instructor approval email through the SCI Permission Number Request portal ↗ or the SCI Permission Qualtrics Form ↗ (TODO: confirm current preferred SCI form with the ISP administrator).

3. Courses outside SCI (confirm current departmental contacts): For Biomedical Informatics courses, contact Toni Porterfield with instructor approval, your 7-digit PeopleSoft ID, and the 5-digit class number. For Statistics courses, submit the Department of Statistics permission form selecting “Department Consent Required” with instructor approval attached. For Health Informatics, contact Lori Kieffer and Mel Newcity; for the School of Law, contact Jaime Horensky; for Linguistics, contact Amber L. Fontenot (alf164@pitt.edu) or Jennifer Ann Berkebile (JES379@pitt.edu).

4. Course substitutions, transfer credits & PCHE cross-registration (CMU and regional universities): Obtain written approval from your ISP faculty advisor and ISP Director Peter Brusilovsky (copying Heidi Davis) before registering.
