Doctor of Philosophy

Pursue research and doctoral study in intelligent systems.

Planning guidance. Requirements depend on the catalog that applies to your enrollment. Before making academic decisions, confirm your catalog year and requirements with your advisor or contact the ISP administrator.
Read official PhD requirements ↗

Research and advising

Work with an ISP faculty advisor to plan your research and program milestones. Discuss advisor changes with the ISP administrator and complete the required form.

Milestones and annual review

Discuss preliminary research, comprehensive examinations, dissertation study, and annual evaluations with your advisor to plan your next milestone. Prepare your self-evaluation and review your Academic Advising Report with your advisor.

ISP forums

Forums connect students with research in the ISP community. Refer to invitations from the ISP administrator for current dates, locations, and speaker details.

Dissertation and summer enrollment

Discuss eligibility for Fulltime Dissertation Study and Summer Milestone Study with your advisor and the ISP administrator before requesting enrollment.
