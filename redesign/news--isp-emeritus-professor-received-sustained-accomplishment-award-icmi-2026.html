ISP Emeritus Professor Received Sustained Accomplishment Award at ICMI 2026

Archived ISP news and events.

Back to news

Historical archive. Dates and descriptions refer to the original publication, not upcoming events or current announcements.

Jeffrey Cohn, Professor Emeritus of Psychology and Intelligent Systems, received the Sustained Accomplishment Award at the 28th ACM International Conference on Multimodal Interaction (ICMI 2026). 

Held this year in Napoli, ICMI is a leading international forum that brings together researchers in AI, multimodal data processing, and human interaction to build innovative, human-centered interactive systems that benefit society. This year’s theme was “Context and Cultural Awareness for Multimodal Interaction.”

As part of receiving this award, Cohn gave a keynote address at the forum titled “Forty Years of Research in Multimodal Interaction.” In this talk, he shared what he learned from his experiences over the course of his career that led to him receiving the award.

Please join us in congratulating Dr. Cohn on this achievement!
