ISP Alumnus Receives Fullbright Scholarship to Further Educational Technology in Chile

Archived ISP community and research news story.

Back to news

Historical archive. Dates and descriptions refer to the original publication, not upcoming events or current announcements.

The goal of the Fulbright project is to help further the development of educational technology in Chile, both as a research discipline and as a practical instructional tool for schools. Professor McLaren Scholarship was awarded for him to teach a course on Educational Technology at the Universidad Téchnica Federico Santa Maria in Valparaiso, Chile.
