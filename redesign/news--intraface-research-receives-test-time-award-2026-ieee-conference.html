IntraFace Research Receives Test of Time Award at 2026 IEEE Conference

Content from the supplied ISP website snapshot.

Back to news

Historical archive from the supplied ISP website snapshot. Dates and descriptions refer to the original publication; this is not a current announcement or live feed.

A 2015 research paper titled “IntraFace” received the Test of Time Award at the 2026 IEEE International Conference on Automatic Face and Gesture Recognition, recognizing its lasting impact on the field of computer vision. Jeffery Cohn, Professor Emeritus of Psychology and Intelligent Systems, as the Emeritus co-authored the paper with Fernando De la Torre, Wen-Shen Chu, Xuehan Xiong, Francisco Vicente and Xiaoyu Ding. The paper introduced a free, publicly available software package designed to analyze facial features and behaviors in video.

IntraFace combines several capabilities, including facial feature tracking, head pose estimation, facial attribute recognition and facial expression analysis. The software also introduced a technique for detecting synchronized facial behaviors between two or more people, allowing researchers to identify patterns in how individuals express emotions together. The team demonstrated the software’s potential by measuring audience reactions to a talk and detecting synchronized smiling during parent-infant interactions.

The paper’s recognition at the 2026 conference highlights the continued relevance of its contributions to facial image analysis and its applications in understanding human behavior. By making these tools publicly available, the research helped expand access to automated facial analysis capabilities for researchers and developers.

Please join us in congratulating Dr. Cohn on these achievements!
