Student resources

Advising, registration, and practical guidance for MS and PhD students.

Planning guidance. Requirements depend on the catalog that applies to your enrollment. Before making academic decisions, confirm your catalog year and requirements with your advisor or contact the ISP administrator (hld46@pitt.edu, 6127 Sennott Square, in-office Tuesday/Friday).

Registration, advising, and policies

Lift an advising hold
  1. Review your degree requirements and discuss your term course selection with your ISP faculty advisor.
  2. Email the ISP administrator, Heidi Davis (hld46@pitt.edu), with your advisor’s written approval and copy your advisor on the email.
  3. Enroll in your classes once the advising hold has been lifted (this process applies every term).

Find the ISP administrator ↗

Request a course permission number

Obtain written instructor approval first, then include your 7-digit PeopleSoft ID, the 5-digit class number, and the instructor’s email approval with your request:

  • SCI courses (including FTDJ and SMSJ): Submit an SCI permission number request ↗ and attach the instructor’s approval email.
  • Outside SCI (from the 2026 program guides — confirm current contacts with the offering department): Bioinformatics (Toni Porterfield); Health Informatics (Lori Kieffer & Mel Newcity); Law School (Jaime Horensky); Linguistics (Amber L. Fontenot, alf164@pitt.edu, or Jennifer Ann Berkebile, JES379@pitt.edu); Statistics (submit via the Department of Statistics website with “Department Consent Required” and instructor approval attached).
Retrieve your Academic Advising Report (AAR)
  1. Sign in to My Pitt ↗ and select the PeopleSoft / HighPoint CX icon.
  2. Choose Academics on the left menu, then select Degree Progress.
  3. Select View PDF near the top of the page to download and review your AAR before annual evaluations or graduation.
Course substitutions and transfer credits
  • Course substitutions: If you and your advisor agree on substituting a course for a required course, email ISP Director Peter Brusilovsky and Heidi Davis for permission before registering. After receiving your grade, notify Heidi Davis so she can submit a waiver to SCI Records.
  • Transferring credits: Request the graduate Transfer Request Form from Heidi Davis, then email the completed form and course syllabus to Peter Brusilovsky (copying Heidi Davis) indicating which requirement it applies to. Official transcripts must also be sent to scirecords@pitt.edu.
Travel grants and research computing

ISP Student Travel Grant: Per the 2026 program guides, ISP students may apply once per academic year for $750–$1,000 in travel support (based on need) to present an accepted paper or poster at an AI-related conference (confirm current funding availability with the ISP administrator before booking travel). Email the following items to the ISP director and administrator at least one month before the conference:

  1. Name and date of the conference.
  2. Copy of the paper or poster acceptance letter/email.
  3. Email from your advisor approving your participation.

After returning from an approved trip, work with Bobbie Coyne for reimbursement, and plan to present your work at an upcoming ISP Forum.

Research computing & tech support: For high-performance computing beyond course laptops or lab servers, ask your advisor or PI about a sponsored account with Pitt’s Center for Research Computing and Data (CRCD). For SCI-specific technical assistance, email the SCI Tech Team at scitech@pitt.edu.

Change your advisor

Discuss the change with your prospective ISP faculty advisor, obtain their approval prior to filing, and complete the Graduate Change of Advisor form (PDF) ↗. Copy Heidi Davis on all correspondence related to changing advisors.

Take a course at another university (PCHE cross-registration)

Cross-registration through the Pittsburgh Council on Higher Education (PCHE) lets full-time students enroll at participating regional institutions (including Carnegie Mellon University, Duquesne University, Carlow University, Chatham University, and others). Prior to enrollment, email your advisor, the ISP Director, and Heidi Davis to obtain approval for the course to count toward your degree. After completing the course, send your grade information to Heidi Davis so she can submit a waiver to SCI Records.

Prepare for graduation

Retrieve your Academic Advising Report (AAR) to verify all requirements are satisfied and contact Heidi Davis with any questions. Applying for graduation initiates the certification process at both the ISP program level and SCI Records.
