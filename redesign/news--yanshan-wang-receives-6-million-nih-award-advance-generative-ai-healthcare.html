Yanshan Wang Receives $6 Million NIH Award to Advance Generative AI in Healthcare

Archived ISP news and events.

Back to news

Historical archive. Dates and descriptions refer to the original publication, not upcoming events or current announcements.

Yanshan Wang, associate professor and vice chair for research in the University of Pittsburgh School of Medicine’s Department of Biomedical Informatics with a secondary appointment in the Intelligent Systems Program, is a multiple principal investigator on a new five-year, approximately $6 million award from the National Institutes of Health (NIH). Funded through the National Center for Advancing Translational Sciences (NCATS), the multi-institutional project aims to improve how generative AI can be used to translate clinical practice guidelines into formats that computers can interpret and apply across healthcare systems.

The project showcases a collaboration of researchers from Cornell University and Weill Cornell Medicine, Columbia University, the University of Pittsburgh, UT Southwestern Medical Center and Virginia Commonwealth University. Together, the team will develop a framework for using generative AI to support clinical decision-making while prioritizing trustworthiness, scalability, portability and ethical implementation.

By making clinical guidelines easier to translate into computer-interpretable formats, the research aims to help healthcare systems integrate evidence-based recommendations into clinical workflows. The project could advance the next generation of AI-powered clinical decision support tools, helping bridge the gap between medical knowledge and its practical application in patient care.

Please join us in congratulating Dr. Wang on this achievement!
