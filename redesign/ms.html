Master of Science

Build your foundation in intelligent systems and applied AI.

Planning guidance. Requirements depend on the catalog that applies to your enrollment. Before making academic decisions, confirm your catalog year and requirements with your advisor or contact the ISP administrator.
Read official MS requirements ↗

Plan your study

Meet with your advisor to plan your courses and build your core skills before selecting advanced electives.

Your MS project

Connect with an ISP faculty advisor early to discuss a research or practical project. Discuss the project report, presentation, and committee requirements with your advisor before planning your project.

Before inviting committee members: ask your advisor and the ISP administrator to confirm the committee composition that applies to your project.

Prepare to graduate

Review your Academic Advising Report and discuss any missing requirements with the ISP administrator before applying for graduation.
