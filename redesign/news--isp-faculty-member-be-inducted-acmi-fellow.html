ISP Faculty Member to be Inducted into the ACMI as a Fellow

Content from the supplied ISP website snapshot.

Back to news

Historical archive from the supplied ISP website snapshot. Dates and descriptions refer to the original publication; this is not a current announcement or live feed.

Dr. Shyam Visweswaran (professor, Department of Biomedical Informatics) will be inducted into the American College of Medical Informatics (ACMI) as a fellow. ACMI is an international honorary college of elected informatics fellows who have made notable contributions to medical informatics and have been meticulously peer reviewed.

Visweswaran will be inducted with 22 other new fellows on Sunday, Nov. 12 at a dinner during the American Medical Informatics Association 2023 Annual Symposium.

Read more about the inductees here.

Please join us in congratulating Dr. Visweswaran on this achievement!
