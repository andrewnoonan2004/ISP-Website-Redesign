Registration and advising

Lift an advising hold
  1. Discuss your course selection with your advisor.
  2. Email the ISP administrator with your advisor’s approval and copy your advisor.
  3. Enroll after the advising hold has been lifted.

Find the ISP administrator ↗

Request a course permission number

Obtain written instructor approval. Include your PeopleSoft ID, the class number, and the instructor’s approval with your request. Courses outside SCI use their school or department’s process.

Review your Academic Advising Report
  1. Sign in to My Pitt and open PeopleSoft / HighPoint Campus Experience.
  2. Choose Academics, then Degree Progress.
  3. Select View PDF to download your report.

Course substitutions and transfer credits

Discuss the request with your advisor and obtain program approval before counting a substitution or transfer toward your degree. Contact the ISP administrator for forms and records processing.

Travel and research support

Contact your advisor and the ISP administrator about conference travel support, research computing, and required approvals before making arrangements.

Change your advisor

Discuss the change with your prospective advisor, obtain their approval, and complete the Graduate Change of Advisor form. Copy the ISP administrator on related correspondence.

Open the change of advisor form (PDF)

Take a course at another university

Discuss Pittsburgh Council on Higher Education cross-registration with the ISP administrator before enrolling. PhD students should obtain approval from their advisor and the ISP director. After completing the course, send the grade information to the administrator for records processing.

Prepare for graduation

Review your Academic Advising Report for missing requirements and contact the ISP administrator with questions. Applying for graduation starts the program and SCI Records certification process.
