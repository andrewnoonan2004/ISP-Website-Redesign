Curriculum tracks and academic standing

The 2026 MS program guide outlines two curriculum tracks: the General Intelligent Systems Track and the Biomedical Informatics Track. Per the program guide (confirm your applicable catalog year), students complete at least 30 credits, earn a grade of B- or better in each required curriculum course, maintain an overall QPA of at least 3.0, and complete an MS project.

Advising and course planning

New MS students are generally assigned to the ISP Director (Peter Brusilovsky) as their initial advisor for the first year and may switch to any ISP faculty advisor by obtaining their approval and submitting the Graduate Change of Advisor form (PDF) ↗ (copying the ISP administrator). Meet with your advisor each term before registration so the ISP administrator can lift your advising hold.

Your MS project and ISSP 2000

The MS project is a faculty-advised research or practical AI project—not a formal MS thesis (publishing a paper is encouraged but not required). It comprises:

  1. Significant research, design, or development work demonstrating your skills with modern AI technologies.
  2. A written project report.
  3. An oral defense/presentation before your three-member faculty committee, chaired by your ISP faculty advisor.

Start early: most students finish the project in their final semester, but waiting until the final semester to begin often delays graduation. You may optionally register for ISSP 2000 (MS Research) with your advisor to allocate credit hours toward project work (the program guide recommends taking no more than two regular lecture courses alongside 3 credits of ISSP 2000 during an intensive project semester; ISSP 2000 does not count as an elective).

Before inviting committee members: the 2026 MS guide contains two different statements on whether two or all three committee members must be ISP faculty (TODO: confirm). Ask your advisor and the ISP administrator to confirm the committee composition that applies to your project before inviting members.

ISP forums and prepare to graduate

MS students are invited to attend ISP Forums on alternating Fridays from 12:30–1:30 p.m. in 5317 Sennott Square. After completing your project defense, email Heidi Davis so she can route the Report on Examinations form for committee signatures and submit it to SCI Records. Review your Academic Advising Report (AAR) before applying for graduation.
