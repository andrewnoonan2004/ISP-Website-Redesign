Applied AI across disciplines

The Intelligent Systems Program (ISP) is a multidisciplinary graduate program at the University of Pittsburgh dedicated to applied artificial intelligence. Founded in 1986 and housed in the School of Computing and Information (SCI) ↗, ISP connects faculty and graduate students across computing, biomedical informatics, law, education, engineering, and the arts and sciences.
