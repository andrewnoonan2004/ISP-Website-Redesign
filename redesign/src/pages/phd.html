Curriculum tracks and academic standing

The 2026 PhD program guide outlines two tracks: the General Intelligent Systems Track and the Biomedical Informatics Track. Per the program guide (confirm your applicable catalog year), doctoral students complete at least 72 credits (coursework and research), earn a grade of B- or better in each required curriculum course, and maintain a QPA of at least 3.0 in full (non-provisional) standing.

Research and advising

New students are generally assigned to the ISP Director (Peter Brusilovsky) for their first year. Your ongoing advisor must be a member of the ISP faculty. To change advisors, obtain approval from your prospective advisor and submit the Graduate Change of Advisor form (PDF) ↗, copying the ISP administrator (hld46@pitt.edu).

Milestones and annual review

Work with your advisor to plan your preliminary examination project (developed across ISSP 2020 and ISSP 2030 with a 30-minute Spring ISP Forum presentation), comprehensive examination, dissertation proposal, and defense. Each year, students submit a self-evaluation and review their Academic Advising Report (AAR) alongside their advisor’s evaluation; the ISP faculty and Director review progress and send an annual summary letter. Under University statute of limitations rules, PhD requirements must be completed within 10 years of initial registration (or 8 years if awarded credit for an appropriate Master’s degree).

ISP forums

ISP Forums take place on alternating Fridays from 12:30–1:30 p.m. in 5317 Sennott Square. To remain in good standing and maintain eligibility for Provost Fellowships, SCI funding, summer GSA funding, and ISP Travel Grants, PhD students are expected to attend and sign in at all but two (2) scheduled Forum talks per term (and present at least once a year, or after receiving an ISP Travel Grant). Notify the ISP director and administrator in advance if extenuating circumstances prevent attendance.

Dissertation and summer milestone study (FTDJ & SMSJ)

  • Full-Time Dissertation Study (FTDJ): Provides full-time status at reduced fee levels once you have completed 72 degree credits, finished all required courses, and are working toward your Comprehensive Examination.
  • Summer Milestone Study (SMSJ): Supports PhD students working on milestones over the summer who are not yet eligible for FTDJ.

Confirm eligibility with your advisor and the ISP administrator before requesting a permission number ↗.
