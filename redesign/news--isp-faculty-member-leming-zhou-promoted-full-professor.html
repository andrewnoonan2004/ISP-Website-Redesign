ISP Faculty Member Leming Zhou Promoted to Full Professor

Archived ISP community and research news story.

Back to news

Historical archive. Dates and descriptions refer to the original publication, not upcoming events or current announcements.

Leming Zhou, a faculty member for ISP as well as the Department of Health Information Management within Pitt's School of Health and Rehabilitation Sciences, was recently promoted from associate professor to full professor. 

Dr. Zhou is also the Director of the Master of Science in Health Informatics (MSHI) program, and has secondary appointments in the Department of Bioengineering in the School of Engineering and McGowan Institute for Regenerative Medicine.

Please join us in congratulating Dr. Zhou on this achievement!
