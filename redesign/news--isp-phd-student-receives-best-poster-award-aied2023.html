ISP PhD Student Receives Best Poster Award at AIED2023

Content from the supplied ISP website snapshot.

Back to news

Historical archive from the supplied ISP website snapshot. Dates and descriptions refer to the original publication; this is not a current announcement or live feed.

ISP PhD student Yuya Asano, SCI alum Mingzhi Yu (SCI ’20), as well as SCI faculty members Professor Diane Litman, Associate Professor Adriana Kovashka, and Associate Professor Erin Walker received the Best Poster Award at the 24th International Conference on Artificial Intelligence in Education.

They received the award alongside Nikki Lobczowski, a postdoctoral associate in the University of Pittsburgh’s Learning Research & Development Center, and Professor Timothy Nokes-Malach of Pitt’s Department of Psychology, for their paper “Impact of Experiencing Misrecognition by Teachable Agents on Learning and Rapport.”

Join us in congratulating Yuya and Mingzhi, as well as Drs. Litman, Kovashka, and Walker on this achievement!
